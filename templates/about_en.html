OTASEC — OT Asset Security — collects operational-technology and industrial-control-system security news in one place, for the people who own, run and protect industrial assets.

How it works

  1. Collect. Every hour OTASEC reads dedicated OT/ICS outlets, vendor and government advisories, the broader security press and selected posts on X.
  2. Filter and tag. Claude, Anthropic's AI model, checks each item for a real OT/ICS or critical-infrastructure angle and tags it by type (advisory, incident, regulation…), sector and vendor. It also writes a one-line summary in English and Dutch. General IT news without an industrial angle is left out.
  3. Brief. Every morning the day's stories are clustered. The ones covered by the most independent sources lead the briefing, each with a short summary, a practical takeaway for asset owners and links to every source.

Editorial safeguards

  • Every link points to an article that was actually fetched. The AI can't add links of its own.
  • Summaries are machine-generated from each source's own title and excerpt. Always read the original before acting, especially on advisories.
  • OTASEC republishes no full articles. It links to them.
